Discover Our Purpose

About The Club

Vision

To empower students to explore and master automation technologies through UiPath, fostering innovative thinking and practical problem-solving in real-world processes. We aim to create a community where automation drives efficiency, creativity, and positive impact across industries and society.

Mission

To provide students with hands-on experience in RPA and UiPath, enabling them to design and implement intelligent automation solutions.

To develop teamwork, technical skills, and leadership through mentor-led workshops and projects.

To inspire innovation and a passion for automation by organizing events, hackathons, and training programs.

To create solutions that enhance productivity, optimize workflows, and contribute to societal and industrial growth.

Objectives

1

To equip students with industry-ready skills in UiPath and automation technologies.

2

To encourage participation in automation challenges, competitions, and techfests to foster excellence.

3

To promote awareness of RPA among young minds and inspire them to leverage automation for positive change.

4

To support the development of innovative automation projects addressing business and societal challenges.

Membership

Join Us

Be part of Sairam UiAutomistix Club and explore the world of automation.

Who Can Join?

Open to all students of Sri Sairam Engineering College who are passionate about automation, RPA, UiPath, robotics, technology, innovation, and problem-solving. No prior experience is required — just curiosity and a drive to learn.

Membership Benefits

Hands-On Automation

Get practical exposure to automation technologies including RPA, UiPath, and intelligent process automation.

Workshops & Training

Attend skill-building workshops, bootcamps, and training sessions conducted by experienced members and industry professionals.

Project Experience

Collaborate on real-world automation projects, build your portfolio, and apply your knowledge in meaningful ways.

Team & Community

Work alongside like-minded peers, build lasting connections, and grow as part of a vibrant technology community.

Leadership Growth

Develop leadership, communication, and organizational skills through active roles within MAGIC and SCOPE frameworks.

Events & Competitions

Represent the club in inter-college competitions, hackathons, idea pitches, and technical events.

How to Join

1

Explore the Club

Learn about Sairam UiAutomistix Club, our activities, events, and what we do through this website.

2

Fill the Registration Form

Complete the official membership registration form with your name, department, year, and contact details.

3

Submit Your Details

Review and submit the registration form. Ensure all information is accurate before submitting.

4

Await Club Communication

The club will reach out with further instructions, orientation details, and onboarding information.
